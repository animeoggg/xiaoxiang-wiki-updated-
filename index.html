Overview

Xiaoxiang Sects Expanded (shown in game as Xiaoxiang Sects & Beasts) is an unofficial addon for Xiaoxiang Cultivation World. It turns the main mod's world into a living cultivation world:

  • Harder cultivation: cultivation is slow, breakthroughs are risky, resources and techniques are scarce, and stronger cultivators are a real threat to weaker ones.
  • The wilds: demon beasts of the Classic of Mountains and Seas that cultivate, cast spells and fly. There are spirit stone caves and herb grottos, beast nests, world bosses and ten great evils sealed under altars.
  • Mortal kingdoms: walled villages, towns and capitals with kings, clans, shops, inns, arenas and gambling houses. Towns are split into slums, an outer ring and an inner ring, and each has its own underworld.
  • Clans and sects: clan estates out in the wilds, each with its own craft. Generated sects are ranked and specialised, and the finest of them are Holy Sects with pavilions far above any clan's.
  • Your own sect: recruit disciples, raise buildings, run production, keep protection arrays, and defend against beast tides, war parties, sieges and demonic invasions. You can also take on missions, form alliances, fight wars, take vassals and hold tournaments.
  • Your inner world: the Dantian World (meridians, storage, Dao seeds and the Dantian Guard), battles in the Soul Sea, and Domain Expansion.
  • Missions: a Mission Journal with thirty kinds of missions, story chains, requests from the people you meet, reputation, achievements and titles.

The demon beasts come from Threateningly Mobs. Each creature keeps its own body and attacks, and gains a cultivation realm, a nature, spells and techniques.

Installation

  1. Install Forge 1.20.1.
  2. Put the Xiaoxiang Cultivation World jar, the Threateningly Mobs jar and the addon jar in your mods folder. Keep only one addon jar: remove the old one when you update.
  3. Install the same addon version on the client and the server. The network version must match (it is currently 24).
  4. A new world is strongly recommended. Caves, settlements, clan estates, sect arrays, grottos and secret realm layouts only appear in newly generated land.

What the addon changes in the base game

  • Spawning: only creatures that cultivate spawn by themselves. Vanilla monsters and animals no longer spawn naturally, but spawners, eggs, breeding and commands still work.
  • Wild spawns follow your strength: creatures that spawn by themselves are matched to the strongest player nearby (see The Harsh World).
  • Villages: vanilla villages and villagers are replaced by the mortal kingdoms' walled settlements.
  • Spirit stones: spirit stone ore and spirit vein springs no longer generate in the open world. Ore is found only in the caves.
  • Threateningly Mobs structures (palaces, nests, altars, dungeons) never generate. Its creatures remain.
  • Arrays: spirit vein cores and formation core plates store and supply far more qi, so protection arrays can stay up.
  • Crafting by hand: the alchemy core, refining core and formation plate only work through hands-on minigames, and talismans are sealed with a brush minigame.
  • Scarcity: breakthrough materials (Foundation and Golden Core) are rare in chests, rare with wandering cultivators and sold at four times the price.
  • A harsh world (on by default): half-speed cultivation, harder breakthroughs, dearer goods, thinner chests and realm suppression. All of it can be configured.

Compatibility

The addon hooks the main mod with small, optional mixins and never changes the main mod's jar. If a future main mod update changes the code a hook relies on, that hook switches itself off quietly instead of crashing.

Getting Started

A suggested path for a new world. You don't have to follow it in this order.

Your first days (Mortal to Qi Refining)

  • Find a settlement. Mortal towns sell food, pills, herbs, arms and talisman supplies, and their inns, guilds and arenas have work. Use /sectcity nearest to find one.
  • Wild beasts are matched to you. While you're weak, the beasts that spawn near you stay within one realm of you, and boss-rank beasts don't roam near you until you reach Foundation. Beyond 10,000 blocks from spawn the wilds are not matched to you at all.
  • Talk to people: sneak and right-click with an empty hand. Wandering cultivators give news, advice and requests. Townsfolk, clan disciples, sect disciples and even wild beasts can be talked to.
  • The Mission Journal (O) lists daily missions and the Mission Board. The Missions window on the left of the screen tracks what you're doing.
  • The Dao Awakening: when you first draw qi into your body, the heavens choose your Dao Path (Sword, Pill, Body or Demonic). Your path grows with deeds that suit it.
  • Spirit stones come from caves, slain beasts, bounties, missions and trade. The first wild caves are Low-grade.

Building strength (Foundation to Golden Core)

  • Breakthroughs need scarce materials. The Paths to Ascension (cultivation menu, G) list every road to them: your sect's Scripture Pavilion, the sect trial, a master's teaching, an ally's exchange, comprehension under a storm, a royal grant, clan adoption and more.
  • Found a sect with a Sect Master's Command Token, recruit wandering cultivators and raise buildings.
  • Tame a companion beast with a Beast Subduing Talisman.
  • Join a Holy Sect by winning its recruitment fair in a capital under its sway.
  • Explore spirit stone caves of higher grades, secret realm gates, spirit vein grottos and clan estates.

Becoming a power (Nascent Soul and beyond)

  • Domain Expansion (V) unfolds from Golden Core (incomplete), sealed from Nascent Soul and open from Mahayana.
  • The great breakthroughs beyond Golden Core cost Ascension Seals, Beast Soul Orbs and spirit stones.
  • Take on the great powers: world bosses, the great evils at the Demon-Sealing Altar, S-Rank gates, wars, sieges and vassals.
  • Beyond Earth Immortal, the addon opens its own road through the immortal realms, and an immortal master can fold the sect into a Sect Domain of its own.

FAQ and Known Limitations

Where do I find spirit stones? Caves (ore and chests), slain beasts, bounties, missions, miners, veins, vassals, tributes, trade and the money changers. They don't generate as ore in the open world.

Everything is too hard / too easy. The harsh world is on by default. Ease it with /harshworld speed 0.75 and /harshworld predators 10, or turn it off with /harshworld off. Wild spawns are matched to your strength by [wild_scaling].

I keep getting one-shot at the start. Make sure [wild_scaling] enabled = true. Within 10,000 blocks of spawn, wild beasts stay within one realm of the strongest player near them, and bosses wait until Foundation. Structures, nests, tides and realms are not matched.

My array runs out of stones. From 1.0.147 a reservoir pays an array's running cost for free and spends stones only to repair damage. Keep Spirit Vein Cores in its reach for free qi.

Placing a Spirit Vein Core turned it into a reservoir. That was the old behaviour. From 1.0.141, conversion happens only on a sneaking, empty-handed right-click (or never, with vein_core_conversion = OFF). Sneak + right-click a reservoir to turn it back.

My alchemy core, refining core or formation plate does nothing. Every craft goes through a minigame. Choose the recipe, press the core's button, then play.

I can't enter a clan estate. Its array lets through anyone the clan doesn't count as an enemy (regard above −15). The rings inside ask fees and regard.

The game lags when new land loads. Freezes marked Qingqiu region … planned … urgent=true in the log come from Xiaoxiang Cultivation World planning its fox-spirit lands in brand-new terrain. They're worst after a long teleport into unexplored land, and the addon can't change them.

Do I need a new world? Strongly recommended. Caves, settlements, wards, clan estates, sect arrays, grottos and realms appear only in newly generated land. Settlements already recorded keep their old layout so the built parts still match.

Something crashed. Send latest.log (or the crash report).

Known limitations

  • Interrupted battles: a bout, tide, siege, raid or minigame interrupted by a restart ends quietly. A bout replays, and the materials for an unfinished craft are returned.
  • Online only: bounty hunters, sieges, invasions and kidnappings need the player online.
  • Main mod updates: if the main mod changes code a hook relies on, that hook switches off quietly. Report it so the addon can be updated.
  • Inner Heaven: the old Inner Heaven island (1.0.124–1.0.127) is retired. Its treasures moved into the Dantian World.

Cultivation

The Harsh World

Cultivation is hard, the means to it are scarce, and the strong rule the weak. On by default. Every part can be tuned under [harsh_world] (see Server Settings) or live with /harshworld (operators).

Cultivating is slow

  • Half speed: cultivation from absorbing qi and meditating is halved (cultivation_speed 0.5). Pills are not affected.
  • Breakthroughs: every breakthrough chance loses 15 percentage points (never below 1%). Guaranteed breakthroughs are unaffected.
  • The cultivation mod's own difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard or Inferno) stacks with all of this.

Resources and techniques are earned

  • Prices: cultivation goods cost ×1.5 in every addon shop, pavilion, clan counter and back room. Mortal goods are unchanged.
  • Techniques: wandering cultivators carry spell books and technique manuals only 35% as often, one at a time. They teach you one of their arts only at 90 regard.
  • Chests: in every chest loot table of the world, each stack of cultivation goods is kept only 40% of the time and halved. Spell books and manuals are kept at the technique rarity. Mortal loot is untouched, and so are earned rewards (secret realm spoils, boss drops, quests, bounties, heirlooms). Generated sects' storehouses are thinned the same way.
  • Breakthrough materials (Foundation and Golden Core) are always scarce, harsh world or not:
    • The Dao Foundation Fruit and Foundation Secret are not found in ordinary chests.
    • Sect storehouses keep at most one of a kind.
    • Wandering cultivators rarely carry them, and sell them at four times the price.

The strong rule the weak

  • Realm suppression: in a fight between cultivators (players and cultivator NPCs):
    • the stronger one's blows hit the weaker +40% per great realm of difference, up to ×4;
    • the weaker one's blows on the stronger keep 70% per great realm (70%, 49%, 34%…), never below 15%.
  • Realm pressure: within 14 blocks of a cultivator two or more great realms above you, their aura gives you Slowness and Weakness (Mining Fatigue at three realms or more). Your own sect's people never press on you.
  • Predators: a loose cultivator two or more great realms above you, met in the wilds, may decide you are prey: 20%, more if they dislike you, less if they like you. Each checks once every 10 minutes. They either:
    • demand your spirit stones: sneak within 10 seconds to kneel and pay a third of what you carry, or they attack; or
    • attack outright.
  • No predators inside town walls, clan estates or your own sect's grounds. Townsfolk, clan folk, sect members, avengers and examiners never prey on you.

The wilds are matched to you

Creatures that spawn by themselves are matched to the strongest player within 128 blocks. Settings are under [wild_scaling].

  • Demon beasts: a wild demon beast is at most one great realm above that player. A Mortal or Qi Refining player meets Qi Refining and Foundation beasts.
  • Bosses: boss-rank beasts roam only near players of Foundation or higher.
  • Other monsters (other mods' and the cultivation mod's own) spawning naturally near a Mortal or Qi Refining player may have at most 60 health. The limit grows ×2.5 with each great realm. Vanilla monsters are never affected.
  • Full danger stays in structures, nests, beast tides, warbands, secret realms, at the Demon-Sealing Altar, and beyond 10,000 blocks from the world spawn.

Breakthroughs and Foundation

Consolidation

After every stage you rise, a breakthrough consolidation minigame opens: steer the surging qi orb and keep it inside the shrinking circle.

  • 80% or more: +3% cultivation, plus Strength and Regeneration for 5 minutes.
  • 50% or more: +1%.
  • Below 50%: qi deviation (weakened and slowed).

Foundation quality

How cleanly you consolidated each breakthrough, together with the grade of your Foundation and Golden Core Daos, gives your foundation quality (0–100).

Grade Quality
Cracked below 25
Common 25–44
Solid 45–59
Superior 60–74
Flawless 75–89
Heavenly 90+

A sound foundation:

  • adds up to +10 points to every breakthrough chance;
  • softens tribulation lightning (up to −40%);
  • makes the main mod's inner-world trials easier (up to −25% damage taken, +25% dealt);
  • makes consolidation gentler and pays more cultivation from it.

Heart demons and old age

  • Heart demons: after a major breakthrough from Foundation on, a heart demon may take form (45%, always on the Demonic Path). It's a mirror of you, with your realm, weapon and armour, and it pulls you into your Soul Sea.
    • Slay it within 3 minutes: your Dao heart steadies (Strength, Regeneration, a surge of cultivation, Path experience).
    • Fail, die or flee: this realm's progress is lost and you're weakened for a while.
  • Old age: as your bone age nears your lifespan, your body grows frail. Weakness comes from three quarters of your lifespan; Slowness and Mining Fatigue in its last tenth. Each major breakthrough announces your new lifespan. Youth Pills push it back (refined by Master alchemists, found in inheritances and auctions).

The immortal road

The cultivation mod lists the immortal realms but opens no breakthrough into them yet. The addon opens its own road from Earth Immortal, through Heavenly, Perfected, Mystic, Golden, Taiyi Golden and Daluo Golden Immortal.

  • After Daluo the road forks: the Saint path (Quasi-Saint, Saint, Dao Ancestor) or the Hunyuan path (Hunyuan Golden Immortal, Hunyuan Daluo, Hunyuan Wuji Daluo). Both end at Great Dao Saint.
  • Each ascension asks for Ascension Seals, Beast Soul Orbs and spirit stones, all growing with each step (4,000 × step² stones), plus Immortal Resonance.
  • Immortal Resonance comes from clearing Immortal Resonance gates: S-Rank secret realms drawn into resonance when an immortal is within 256 blocks.

Dantian World and Soul Sea

丹田世界 The Dantian World

Your own body as a world. Open it with H or the ☯ Dantian World button on the cultivation menu. It has four tabs.

Meridians (经脉)

Defence and counterplay

  • Qi Ward 护体结界 (Shift+V, Foundation): a domain's sure-hit can't touch you while it holds. It drains 1.5% of your qi a second.
  • Domain Amplification 领域增幅 (B with no domain, Golden Core): your blows strip blessings, shatter Qi Wards and drain a cultivator's qi. Arts and sure-hits strike you at half force. It drains 1.2% qi a second.
  • Clashes: when two domains overlap, a 6-second contest of will begins. Mash V to push. The higher realm leans on the weaker by itself. The loser's domain shatters and they're burnt out for 45 s.
  • Breaking a barrier from outside: a sealed barrier has integrity (60 + 30 per realm). Strike it, or hit it with arrows or spells. At zero it shatters and its master is burnt out for a minute.

When it falls

A domain ends when its time runs out, or early if you step outside it, lose 45% of your health, fall, or lose a clash. You're then burnt out for 30 s (Weakness and Slowness), and your inner world needs 3 minutes to settle. Every block the barrier covered is put back exactly as it was, even after a crash.

Domain mastery

  • Gaining it: mastery (0–10, Unformed to Absolute) grows with every domain unfolded and every foe slain inside one.
  • Each rank: −4% cost, +2 s duration, +3% strength and −10 s cooldown.

Others who unfold domains

  • Your elders: Elders and Grand Elders of Nascent Soul or above unfold sealed domains when the sect is hard pressed (under the Fight order, at most once every 10 minutes).
  • Great evils unfold one below half health.
  • Tide Lords, Siege Marshals and world bosses unfold one in phase III.

Your sect and the towns

A Sect Master can deal with a settlement's ruler:

  • Offer protection: the town pays your sect a daily tribute, and you defend it from bandit raids (the raid chance depends on its might). Your sect enters without toll.
  • Demand surrender: defeat the ruling clan's cultivators (in a capital, the royal guard and the ruling clan) within 10 minutes. You get double tribute, but restless subjects: a conquered town may rise in rebellion unless it is held.
  • Garrison: station disciples who came with you. They keep the gates, the plaza and (in a capital) the castle bridge, fighting beside the town guard. A garrison keeps a conquered town from rising, steadies a protected one, and drives off bandit raids even while you're away.
  • When the cultivation mod's demon sieges strike a town, the kingdom rewards its defenders and mourns a fallen town.

How wild beasts spawn

The addon spawns beasts itself, around each player, so the wilds stay dangerous by day too. By default:

  • Rate: every 6 seconds, 2 nearby spots are tried, each of which can bring a whole pack. At night and underground the rate is 140%; by day on the surface, 80%.
  • Distance and cap: beasts appear 24–56 blocks away, and stop once 14 wild beasts are within 64 blocks of you.
  • Spacing: a rare beast never appears within 40 blocks of another; a boss never within 128 blocks of another.

Spirit Stone Caves and Herb Grottos

Caves are the only source of spirit stone ore. Each is a great open gateway in a rock hill, a stair down to three maze floors, and a huge hall where the Cave Lord waits. Every floor is sealed until its guardians are dead.

Where caves appear

  • Wild caves are always Low-grade. The world is split into regions of 14 × 14 chunks; by default 40% hold one (set in the Almanac). About a third of wild caves are Herb Grottos.
  • Sect caves: every generated sect owns one cave near its grounds, graded by its rank: Mid for Lower sects, High for Middle sects and Supreme for Higher sects.

Elements

Every spirit stone cave has one element: Blazing (fire), Frozen (ice), Tidal (water), Stoneheart (earth), Verdant (wood) or Abyssal (dark).

  • Guardians share its nature. With the default roster they're the myth beasts of that nature, with the elites Luwu, Bashe or Xingtian as Cave Lords.
  • Chests favour technique and spell manuals of that element.
  • Herb Grottos are kept by earth and water beasts only.

  4. Stairwells: a spiral stair around a lit pillar joins the floors.
  5. The Cave Lord's hall: a domed cavern about 53 blocks across, ringed by eight great columns.

Beast Tides

At dusk, a horde of demon beasts may gather against a sect and attack it in three waves.

Which sects are struck

  • Your sect: each dusk there's a chance (20% by default) if the master is online in the sect's world and the sect last had a tide at least 2 days ago.
  • Generated sects: a sect within about 128 blocks of a player can be struck, at half that chance. Defend it and it remembers.
  • Gate breaks: a secret realm gate left uncleared when it closes breaks, and its beasts pour out as a tide.
  • Limits: at most 3 tides at once, never two within 200 blocks of each other.

How a tide unfolds

  1. Warning: a raid horn sounds, and nearby players and your sect are told. Your alarm bell rings itself. The tide strikes 30 seconds later.
  2. Wave 1: common beasts. Wave 2: a heavier mix. Final wave: rare beasts led by a Tide Lord boss.
  3. Where they gather: waves gather outside your grounds and march in. They never spawn inside the array.

Breaking the array

  • Breaking the array is their first job. Beasts head for the nearest stretch of the dome and batter it, ignoring defenders behind the barrier unless they come outside.
  • They bombard it with their strongest ranged arts as they close in (fireballs, Star Fall, flying swords, sword auras, palm thunder and more), and raiders use the main mod's siege-barrier casting.
  • Swarm pressure: each blow counts as the worse of its own damage or a share of the array's whole qi, so big swarms threaten even immortal arrays. A tide of 25 left unanswered breaks an immortal array in about half a minute.
  • Which foes your flags can hold: Low flags hold Qi Refining foes, rising to Immortal flags for Mahayana. Each realm above that hits ×1.5 harder (up to ×4).
  • Blood-burst beasts (red spores above them, about 1 in 4 of the common and uncommon) charge the wall and explode for double damage. Kill them early. Any tide beast at the wall below 30% health blows itself up against the array rather than die for nothing.
  • At 0 qi the array shatters and the main mod's 5-minute recovery begins.
  • The array strikes back with heavenly lightning on beasts hammering it: 1–5 bolts by core grade, each costing 0.8% of its qi. It never spends its last 15%.

Inside the walls

  • Wrecking: with mobGriefing on, beasts smash fragile things (glass, fences, torches, lanterns, crops). They never touch chests or other block entities.
  • Plunder: beasts that reach the headquarters steal spirit stones from the treasury. Kill a looter and its stones go back.
  • If you win, your disciples rebuild everything that was smashed.

Winning and losing

  • Victory when the final wave is wiped out:
    • Your sect: treasury stones, prestige +1 and loyalty for those who fought.
    • A generated sect: stones for every player who helped, and its attitude toward your sect rises by 15.
  • Defeat:
    • If no player stays within 96 blocks for 2 minutes, or the tide isn't broken within 12 minutes, the tide wins.
    • Your sect loses part of its treasury and storehouse.
  • A battle report opens for everyone who fought (see Defending Your Sect).

Companion Beasts

Beat a demon beast down and bind it with a Beast Subduing Talisman, and it becomes your companion. It fights for you, can be ridden (and flown), grows, evolves and lends you its spells.

Taming: the battle of wills

  • Recipe: talisman paper, demon core (tier 1), demon bone and lead → 2 talismans.

The rules of a realm

  • No flying until the realm is conquered.

The production cycle

Everything made goes into the storehouse, and the workshops draw what they need from it:

  • Herbs to pills: Herb Fields (farmers) grow spirit herbs, and the Alchemy Workshop (alchemists) turns them into pills for disciples in Seclusion.
  • Metals and cores to gear: mines (miners) bring metals, and missions and beasts bring demon cores. The Forge Hall (smiths) turns them into weapons, armour and treasures.
  • Fuel: the treasury supplies spirit stones as furnace fuel for both.

A batch is made only when every ingredient is in the storehouse and the fuel in the treasury. What's missing is reported each morning and in the building's Steward's Ledger, where you also assign its workers.

  • Crafts: every trade a disciple works earns craft experience and a rank: Apprentice, Journeyman, Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster. Each rank brings bigger harvests, richer veins, surer and finer pills and blades, and fatter tolls.
  • The sect runs itself:
    • stones the workers bring in go to the treasury;
    • the armoury hands forged gear to the fighting disciples;
    • when the storehouse fills, the steward sells the cheapest surplus.
  • The disciples' exchange: disciples earn merit of their own (duties, missions, kills) and spend it on what they need from the storehouse: healing pills, the breakthrough pill of their realm, manuals that suit their roots, gear. What the storehouse lacks becomes a wish they tell you about.

Binding arrays

Use the main mod's formation core plates and flags to raise protection arrays on your grounds, then claim the plates for your sect with the Command Token.

Requests

Small quests from cultivators on the road, your disciples, your beasts and the wild beasts you parley with: slay so many of a kind (alone, or with a companion beside you), or bring so many of a thing. Up to 4 at once, 3 days each. Kills are counted as you go.

Story chains and escort contracts

From Sect Affairs:

  • Story chains (Quest Journal), one at a time:
    • The Missing Elder: Elder Mo joins your sect as an Elder.
    • The Sealed Demon Beneath the Mountain: its egg is your reward.
    • The Rival Sect's Spy: your elders' loyalty rises.
  • Escort contracts:
    • The ward: a Spirit-Stone Caravan (a laden mule), an Envoy or a Herb Merchant's Wagon must reach a place 300–700 blocks away.
    • Ambushes: bandits and beasts strike every minute or so.
    • Success: bring the ward within 10 blocks of the destination for the purse, plus a bonus per ambush beaten.
    • Failure: if it falls, or you leave it behind for a minute, the contract is lost.

Sect Bounty Board

A board block that posts 5 bounties a day (8 with a Mission Hall) for whoever reads it, scaled to your realm.

  • Recipe: 3 planks / paper, demon bone, paper / 3 planks.
  • When placed, it raises a great notice board around itself: planks between timber posts under a tiled roof, lanterns and pinned notices.
  • Taking bounties: right-click any part of it. Take up to 3 at once; new offers come every morning.

Crafting and items

Minigames

Crafting, sealing, stealing and taming are done by hand. Each opens its own screen, and the score decides the result. A higher profession grade makes a game gentler and its result better: alchemy or refining rank, realm for arrays and locks, foundation quality for breakthroughs. A fitting Dao Path helps a little more.

Crafting gates

The main mod's cores only work through a minigame: choose the recipe in the core's own screen, press its button, then play.

Core Minigame Score effect
Alchemy Core Pill refining: keep the furnace's heat inside the moving band 90%+: chance to rise a grade; below 40%: part of the batch spoils; overheat and it explodes
Refining Core Forging: strike as the glowing beats reach the anvil 90%+: masterwork, a grade higher; below 30%: lesser or cracked
Formation Core Plate Array activation: trace the runes 60%+ activates the array

Inscribe a flag (in the Formation core screen) plays the rune game to make a formation flag. It costs 50, 200, 800, 3,200 or 12,800 stones by grade. A flawless score makes two.
